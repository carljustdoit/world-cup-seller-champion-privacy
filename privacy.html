World Cup Seller Champion is a Chrome extension and website for World Cup ticket buyers and sellers. It helps users read public ticket listings, understand fee math, compare prices, and watch market movement.

What the extension handles

  • Public ticket listings visible on supported ticket pages, such as match, category, section, row, seat, list price, and listing status.
  • Estimated fee math, including buyer checkout price and seller payout.
  • Market scan timestamps used to show new listings, changed prices, and tickets no longer listed.
  • Seat details you type for your own pricing, such as section, row, seat, purchase cost, and target price.
  • Email addresses only when you use Direct Match to post a public FIFA-listed seat or ask a seller to contact you.

Local data

By default, market snapshots, saved games, and seats you type as yours stay in Chrome extension storage on your device.

Shared scans

If you turn on scan sharing, the extension sends public ticket listing data from the scan to our backend. Shared scans help build seller insights such as supply, price changes, new tickets, and tickets that disappeared from the market.

Shared scans do not include your name, email, phone number, FIFA account, Google account, payment details, IP address, purchase cost, target price, or seats you type as your own. We send only public market listings shown by the ticket page plus a random anonymous ID.

Insights access code

After a successful shared scan, the extension may show a 24-hour seller insights access code. We store a hashed version of the code, issue time, expiration time, anonymous visitor ID, scan ID, game ID, and shared seat count. We do not store the plain code.

Direct Match

Direct Match is optional. Sellers can promote a public FIFA-listed seat and enter an email plus a direct ask price. Buyers can enter an email to contact the seller. We store encrypted buyer and seller emails, non-reversible email hashes, public seat listing details, direct ask price, relay status, and request time so the buyer and seller can connect without exposing emails by default.

We do not process payment, transfer tickets, verify either side, or guarantee a deal. Buyers and sellers handle offline payment, transfer, safety, and platform rules themselves.

Website analytics

The main website may use Google Analytics after cookie/privacy notice consent. Google Analytics may receive visit information such as pages viewed, browser/device details, approximate location, and referrer. This is separate from extension scan sharing.

Remote code

The Chrome extension does not load or execute remote JavaScript. Extension code, HTML, CSS, and icons are packaged with the extension. Ticket page responses and backend API responses are treated as data.

Exports

CSV and JSON exports are created only when you choose to download them. You control where exported files are saved and how they are used.

Children

World Cup Seller Champion is not intended for children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Independent tool

World Cup Seller Champion is independent. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by FIFA, the FIFA World Cup, or any official ticketing platform.

Use the tool carefully and follow the rules of the ticket platform you are using.
